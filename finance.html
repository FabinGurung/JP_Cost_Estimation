EXTERNAL AUTHORITY · LABOUR / FIELD RESOURCES

Labour and worker records belong to Field & Desk

Attendance, wage workbooks, worker-level Money & Goods, advances and field-resource evidence are maintained in their existing original Google Sheets and the Fishtail manpower custody. The Cost Estimation website does not operate or replicate worker records.

Only verified, approved project-level labour costs or payment allocations should enter Finance or estimate-versus-actual calculations, with explicit organization, project, work item, source ID and source revision. Historical worker catalogues are preserved in Git history, not advertised as current Finance inputs.

Owns the financial register and controls access. No cross-company consolidation without an approved mapping.

Receives payment evidence linked to its verified project ID. A payer or vendor name is not a project owner.

Holds the editable workbook and its PRE/POST versions. The approved summary is published as a dated snapshot, with a direct link to the source Sheet.

Estimator

BOQ and cost-rate data stay separate from actual payments until an explicit, verified Estimate vs Actual mapping is approved.
