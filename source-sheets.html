Permanent IDs and sharing boundaries

Original Google Sheet IDs are unchanged; existing direct links and computations continue to identify the same files. Project archival folders and version histories stay in their original locations. The source folder is access controlled by Drive permissions, not GitHub Pages.

The former project-folder reader access on both Amrit LIVE workbooks was preserved explicitly after the move. Do not make the entire folder or finance workbook public merely to enable read-only Actions; use approved Workload Identity and source-level permissions.
