Measurement & pricing literature

RICS NRM 2 guides detailed measurement; NRM 1 distinguishes cost planning; IFC and IfcOpenShell support model-to-quantity lineage; W3C PROV-O informs source tracking. These are methodological references, not local statutory approvals.
