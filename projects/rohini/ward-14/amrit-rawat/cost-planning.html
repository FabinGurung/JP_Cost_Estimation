Measurement and contract boundaries

  • Plinth-area rates use built-up square feet, not wall/tile measured area.
  • Trade rates use actual site measurements such as points, installed floor/wall area, running feet and bathrooms.
  • Daily wages are per worker-day, not subcontractor lump sums.
  • Labour only and material + labour are alternative pricing arrangements.
  • Turnkey and grey-civil scopes may overlap with finish/MEP trades; never total them together without a contract scope reconciliation.
  • Painting wall area ≈ 3.5 × plinth is only a rough assumption; use measured actual area for a quotation.
  • Taxes, transport, contractor margin, site conditions, project quantities and material grades require separate verification.
