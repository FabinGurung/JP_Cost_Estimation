2. Your land and construction access

These answers change excavation, transport, drainage, and retaining-wall allowances.

These choices apply site multipliers to earthwork and transport. They do not replace a geotechnical investigation or structural design.

3. Rooms

Room counts help the system estimate doors, plumbing fixtures, electrical points, finishes, and wet areas.

Auto-count rules: doors ≈ main entrance + bedrooms + bathrooms + kitchens + circulation allowance. Windows ≈ room-based daylight opening count. Users can later override these in Engineer view.

4. Structure, walls and roof

Use familiar choices. The technical interpretation stays in the backend.

RCC mode uses benchmark quantity coefficients for concrete, reinforcement, and formwork. Load-bearing mode is intentionally conservative in v0.1 and should be engineer-reviewed.

5. Finish quality, doors and windows

Choose what the house should feel like, not technical specifications.

6. Water, bathrooms and kitchen

The system turns these into sanitary, plumbing, tank, drainage, and kitchen BOQ items.

7. Electricity and optional systems

Simple package choices activate the underlying electrical and low-voltage items.

8. Outside the house

Only include what you actually want in the project scope.
